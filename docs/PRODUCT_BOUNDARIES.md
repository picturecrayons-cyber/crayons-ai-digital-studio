# Product boundaries

- StreamVista: AI media production projects, localization, subtitles, dubbing drafts, QC and deliverable preparation.
- Crayons Bridge: sole owner of rights, licensing, buyer marketplace and distribution authorization.
- Crayons LOOP: consumer OTT and TVOD.

StreamVista must not recreate Bridge onboarding, buyer/rights workflows, licensing, film marketplace or OTT catalogue. No title may be represented as cleared for distribution by StreamVista.

# First paid offer
10-minute film localization pilot. Pricing remains quote-based until real processing, storage, review and delivery costs are measured.

# Evidence policy
Never mark transcription, translation, dubbing, payment, QC or delivery complete without an actual verified backend result. The current UI creates browser-local project records only.
